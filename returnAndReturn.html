Return & Refund Policy

At MBT CARS, we are committed to delivering a premium experience across all our services — from luxury rentals to detailing and financing. We understand that plans can change, and we strive to make our return and refund process as smooth and transparent as possible.

Rental Returns & Cancellations

Cancellations (Short-Term Rentals):

  • Free cancellation up to 48 hours before your scheduled pick-up time.
  • Cancellations made within 48 hours may be subject to a 20% cancellation fee.
  • No-shows or same-day cancellations are non-refundable.

Long-Term Rentals & Lease Plans:

  • A separate agreement will outline the terms of cancellation or early return.
  • Early termination fees may apply depending on the contract.

Vehicle Returns:

  • Vehicles must be returned with the same fuel level, clean interior, and no new damage.
  • Excess mileage or additional cleaning may incur extra charges, detailed in your rental agreement.

Car Wash & Detailing Refunds

  • If you're not satisfied with the result, please notify us within 24 hours.
  • We’ll either re-perform the service at no cost or offer a partial/full refund, depending on the situation.
  • Missed appointments without notice may result in a 50% service charge.

Auto Service & Maintenance Refunds

  • Refunds are assessed on a case-by-case basis.
  • If an issue arises due to a service we performed, we will reinspect and repair the issue free of charge.
  • Refunds may be issued if a problem cannot be resolved to your satisfaction.

Financing & Deposit Refunds

  • Financing deposits are typically non-refundable, unless your application is declined or the vehicle becomes unavailable.
  • Lease or financing refund terms are governed by the individual contract and lending institution.

How to Request a Refund or Return

To initiate a return, refund, or service complaint, please contact us:

  • Phone: +359 898 788 7660
  • Email: mbtrentacar@gmail.com
  • WhatsApp: +359 898 788 7660

Please provide your booking number, service date, and a brief description of the issue.

We value your trust and aim to resolve all issues fairly and promptly.
